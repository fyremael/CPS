  \item refine the phase grid near maxima and minimum gaps.
  \end{enumerate}
\item Aggregate a coupling-risk graph and evaluate candidate interventions.
\item Emit an auditable proposal with the triggering measurements, confidence, expected effect, and rollback condition.
\end{enumerate}

\section{A two-dimensional closed form}

For
\[
A(\phi)=
\begin{bmatrix}
d_1 & re^{i\phi}\\
s & d_2
\end{bmatrix},
\]
the eigenvalues are
\[
\lambda_{\pm}(\phi)
=
\frac{d_1+d_2}{2}
\pm
\frac12\sqrt{(d_1-d_2)^2+4rs e^{i\phi}}.
\]
The circle traced inside the square root is mapped through a branch point.  This simple formula already explains loops, branch exchange, and exceptional sensitivity near a vanishing discriminant.
