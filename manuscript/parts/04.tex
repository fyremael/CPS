\delta z_{t+L}=J_{t+L-1}\cdots J_t\delta z_t.
\]
A second-stage analysis should therefore form a reduced monodromy operator or estimate its action by replaying a short checkpoint window.  A frozen-step warning that disappears in the product may be harmless; a modest local warning that compounds coherently may be serious.

\section{Measurement contracts}

A useful diagnostic separates observables that answer different questions.

\subsection{Asymptotic mode risk}

For discrete-time local maps,
\[
R_{ij}=\max_{\phi}\rho(A^{(ij)}(\phi)).
\]
If \(R_{ij}>1\), some allowed orientation creates a locally expanding mode.  The phase-robust stability margin is
\[
\mu_{ij}=1-R_{ij}.
\]

\subsection{Finite-horizon reserve}

Define
\begin{equation}
G_{ij}(K)=
\max_{\phi}
\max_{1\leq k\leq K}
\norm{A^{(ij)}(\phi)^k}_2.
\label{eq:finite-gain}
\end{equation}
This is the clearest direct answer to the operational question: how large can a perturbation become before the local dynamics have time to contract it?

A resolvent-based Kreiss surrogate provides a complementary and often cheaper screen:
\[
\widehat{\mathcal K}_{ij}
=
\max_{\phi}
\max_{z\in\mathcal G,\,|z|>1}
(|z|-1)\norm{(zI-A^{(ij)}(\phi))^{-1}}_2.
\]
The grid \(\mathcal G\) must be reported; this is a comparative estimator, not a certificate.

\subsection{Mode collision and conditioning}

Define
\[
\Delta_{ij}
=
\min_{\phi,k\neq\ell}
|\lambda_k(\phi)-\lambda_\ell(\phi)|
\]
and
\[
\kappa_{ij}^{\max}
=
\max_{\phi,k}
\frac{\norm{x_k}\norm{y_k}}{|y_k^*x_k|}.
\]
Small gaps identify possible collisions; large condition numbers identify modes whose spectral location is unreliable under perturbation.  Near a defective point these quantities become singular.

\subsection{A coupling-risk graph}

Let the reduced coordinates or blocks be vertices.  Assign each directed coupling a risk score such as
\begin{equation}
\mathcal R_{ij}
=
R_{ij}
+w_G\log(1+G_{ij})
+w_\kappa\log(1+\kappa_{ij}^{\max})
+\frac{w_\Delta}{\Delta_{ij}+\eps}.
\label{eq:risk-score}
\end{equation}
The weights are calibration parameters, not universal constants.  The resulting directed graph is a planning object: it shows which block interactions carry fragile modes or large transient reserves.

\section{From measurement to optimizer planning}

A diagnostic earns its place by changing a decision.  \cps{} therefore defines intervention contracts rather than vague recommendations.

\subsection{Damping}

For candidate damping values \(\gamma\), build the corresponding reduced operators and choose the least value satisfying both
\[
\max_{(i,j)\in\mathcal E}R_{ij}(\gamma)\leq1-\mu_0
\]
and
\[
\max_{(i,j)\in\mathcal E}G_{ij}(K;\gamma)\leq G_0.
\]
The first constraint protects asymptotic local stability; the second protects finite-horizon reserve.  Either alone can be misleading.

\subsection{Momentum}

Momentum changes the augmented operator in \cref{eq:momentum-augmented}.  A planning sweep evaluates candidate \(\beta\) values under matched nominal progress.  A candidate that improves the nominal convergence factor but sharply increases \(G_{ij}\) is an acceleration purchased with inadequate reserve.

\subsection{Preconditioning}
