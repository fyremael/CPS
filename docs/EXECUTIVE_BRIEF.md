# Executive Brief

## Decision

Proceed as a staged research programme. The construction is mathematically coherent, implementable at reduced rank, and differentiated from ordinary Hessian or spectral-radius monitoring. Its commercial or production value remains unproven until it predicts failures earlier than nominal transient-gain baselines and guides interventions under matched compute.

## Distinctive measurement

CPS changes the orientation of one coupling while preserving its strength. It therefore measures coherent interaction fragility rather than marginal magnitude. The signal has two channels:

- spectral motion caused by feedback-cycle phase;
- transient-gain variation caused by interference among paths, even when eigenvalues do not move.

## Immediate implementation priority

Functionalize the complete optimizer state for SGD, momentum, and AdamW. Build rank-32 to rank-128 projected Jacobians from JVPs. Reproduce the exact synthetic suite before training neural systems.

## Kill test

Compare CPS against nominal finite-horizon gain. If phase sweeps add no predictive or intervention value after rank and basis ablations, stop the programme or retain it only as an explanatory visualization.

## Scale policy

Single-GPU work is sufficient through the transformer prototype. Larger compute is justified only after the prediction and intervention gates pass.
